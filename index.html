Norea Square Condominium · Introduction

A New Riverfront Landmark on Koh Norea

Norea Square Condominium rises within Norea City on Koh Norea, developed by the Overseas Cambodian Investment Corporation (OCIC) as part of one of Phnom Penh's newest riverfront districts. The tower is positioned to capture unblocked river, sunrise, city and sunset views across every home, with vertical access provided by five high-speed elevators.

Just across the Norea Twin Dragon Bridge from Koh Pich, Norea Square places residents within easy reach of central Phnom Penh while offering the calmer pace of an emerging waterfront neighbourhood.

Diamond Bay Garden · Introduction

Premium Waterfront Living on Koh Pich

Diamond Bay Garden is an OCIC waterfront residential development on Koh Pich (Diamond Island), set at the confluence of the Mekong, Tonle Sap and Bassac rivers. Tower A — the first of three planned towers — brings 591 premium residences across 39 floors, rising 150 metres above Phnom Penh's riverfront.

The design integrates lush ground gardens, cascading vertical greenery and a serene rooftop sky garden, with more than 1,000 sqm of dedicated hospitality space for residents.

Koh Pich Lifestyle

Life on Diamond Island

Koh Pich is Phnom Penh's established live-work-play waterfront district — home to Koh Pich Central Park, riverside promenades, the Connexion community hub, and a thriving café, retail and F&B scene, all within reach of the capital's landmarks.

  • Set on Koh Pich (Diamond Island), between the Mekong, Tonle Sap and Bassac rivers
  • Steps from over 8 hectares of green space, including Koh Pich Central Park
  • Adjacent to Connexion — cafés, coworking, schools and community events
  • Near AEON Mall and a wide range of cafés and restaurants
  • Within reach of Independence Monument and central Phnom Penh
